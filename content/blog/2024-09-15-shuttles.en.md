---
title: "We offer you the shuttles!"
date: 2024-09-15
---

🚌 Starting this year, the Skibus shuttles, which allow you to travel between the hamlets of the resort, are partly paid for by the passengers. Le Col Roulé has decided to offer you 4 adult passes for your stay (it's free for children)!

Here are our tips to make the most of the slopes during your stay with us:

- If you prefer to take the shuttles, the schedules are posted on the apartment door. As they are not very clear, feel free to contact us for more details on the passing times!
- Another option is to do as we do: we leave our boots and skis in the lockers of a store near the gondolas (1€ per day). This way, we go to the lifts and return to the apartment either by shuttle (5 min) or on foot (15 min). Total freedom!
