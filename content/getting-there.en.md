{{< icon name="car" title="car" >}} {{< icon name="motorcycle" title="motorbike" >}} Valloire town hall likes to say it, to come by car or motorbike from anywhere there is only one traffic light! Indeed, the motorway leads to Saint-Michel-de-Maurienne, then after 25mn of ascent you have arrived.

{{< icon name="train" title="train" >}} {{< icon name="bus" title="bus" >}} By train+bus you just have to go to Saint-Michel-de-Maurienne (also called Saint-Michel Valloire) by TGV, then take a bus to Valloire (book in advance !):

- [TicTacTrip](https://www.tictactrip.eu/) (book train+bus at once)
- [Transdev](https://www.valloire.net/en/commerces-et-services/transdev-savoie-liaison-autobus-depuis-la-gare-st-michel-valloire-372299/)
- [Oùra](https://boutique.oura.com/en/travel) (formerly Cars Région Savoie)
- [Altibus](https://www.altibus.com/en/)
