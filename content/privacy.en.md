*Last updated: 2026-11-22.*

This site is run by Hélène and Charles, owners of the Le Col Roulé apartment in Valloire. For any question about your data: [contact@lecolroule.fr](mailto:contact@lecolroule.fr).

### What we do not do

No tracking cookies, no audience measurement, no advertising, no fonts or scripts loaded from a third-party service. That is why the site has no consent banner.

### What the site records

- **Language choice**: if you pick a language in the menu, a cookie `lcr_lang` (one year) remembers it. It only holds that choice.
- **Technical logs**: the host ([bunny.net](https://bunny.net/privacy/), Slovenia, servers in Europe) may briefly keep the IP address and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.
- **Maps**: a map on display loads its tiles from [OpenStreetMap](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy), which then receives your IP address.

### Newsletter

If you sign up, we receive your e-mail address and the language of the form. It is stored at [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy/) (France), which sends our e-mails, and is only used to send you the blog posts. An anti-robot test ([Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)) loads when the form comes into view. Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link; we keep your address until you unsubscribe.

### Booking

Bookings go through [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2855), whose policy applies. If you write to us, we use your message and address to reply.

### Google Business Profile

We use the Google Business Profile API only to publish the blog posts on the apartment's Google listing, with its owners' account. It gives no access to any data of the people who visit this site.

### Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to [contact@lecolroule.fr](mailto:contact@lecolroule.fr). You can also complain to the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/en/), the French data protection authority.
